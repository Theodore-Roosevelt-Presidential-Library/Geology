# Read the Rocks: sources and fact-check

Check these before the trlibrary.com page goes live. ✅ means confirmed against the named source during the build. ⚠️ means it still needs a check or a second source.

## Primary sources

- **[GRI map]** NPS Geologic Resources Inventory, Digital Geologic-GIS Map of Theodore Roosevelt National Park and Vicinity, plus the ancillary map document `thro_geology.pdf` (2020). https://irma.nps.gov/DataStore/Reference/Profile/1044329
- **[MS-86]** Biek, R.F., and Gonzalez, M.A., 2001, *The Geology of Theodore Roosevelt National Park, Billings and McKenzie Counties, North Dakota*. NDGS Miscellaneous Series 86. https://www.dmr.nd.gov/ndgs/Publication_List/pdf/MISC%20SERIES/MS-86%20w.plates.pdf
- **[NPS-CV]** NPS, Coal Vein Trail guide. https://www.nps.gov/thro/planyourvisit/upload/CoalVeinShort.pdf
- **[NPS-PF]** NPS, Hike the Petrified Forest. https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hike-the-petrified-forest.htm
- **[NPS-GL]** NPS, Glaciers / Glacial Features. https://www.nps.gov/thro/learn/nature/glaciers.htm
- **[MS-87]** Erickson, B.R., 1999, *Fossil Lake Wannagan*, Billings County, North Dakota. NDGS Miscellaneous Series 87. https://www.dmr.nd.gov/ndgs/Publication_List/pdf/MISC%20SERIES/MS-87.pdf
- **[NDGS-LS]** NDGS statewide landslide inventory (59,505 areas, mapped 2016 to early 2023 from aerial photos, NAIP and LiDAR). https://ndgishub.nd.gov/arcgis/rest/services/All_Geology/MapServer/4
- **[TRC]** Theodore Roosevelt Center quote archive (project file `TR-Quotes-TRC-Archive.json`)

## Drive-along notes

## Known gaps

- The detailed park map covers the South and North Units and the Elkhorn-area quadrangles, but not the town of Medora or the Library grounds. Taps there use the coarser state map.
- River Bend Overlook is mentioned but not pinned; no verified coordinates yet.
- Photos are from Wikimedia Commons (public domain and CC BY-SA). Swap in Acquia DAM images when available.
